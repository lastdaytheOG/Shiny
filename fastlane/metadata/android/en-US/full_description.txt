Shiny is a free music player for Android, designed like Apple Music. It plays the YouTube Music catalogue with no ads and no account, and it is open source.

LISTEN
• Now Playing built around the artwork, with Lyrics and Up Next one swipe away
• Lyrics that fill word by word, in time with the music
• Background playback, crossfade, gapless albums and volume levelling
• An equaliser with 8D audio, slowed, nightcore and reverb
• Smart Shuffle on every playlist
• Android Auto and four home-screen widgets

DISCOVER
• A Home that learns what you like, with mixes for the time of day
• Charts for your country and the world
• Recognise the song playing around you from Search, a widget or Quick Settings
• Listening stats

YOUR LIBRARY
• Download songs, albums and playlists to a folder you choose
• Play the audio files already on your phone
• Import your Spotify playlists and Liked Songs
• Export as MP3, back up and restore

TOGETHER
• Listen Together: friends join with a code, a link or a QR code and hear the same moment
• One shared queue with votes, reactions and chat
• Friends, a profile page and a Discord status that opens the song in Shiny

LOOK AND FEEL
• Liquid Glass design with light, dark and AMOLED black
• Pick an accent colour or take it from the artwork
• 50+ interface languages
