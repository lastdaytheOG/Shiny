# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who listen to a lot of music on Android and care how their player looks and feels. Many
come from Apple Music or Spotify and judge Shiny against them. They use it on the move, at a desk,
and with the phone propped up while doing something else (Ambient Mode).

## Product Purpose
Shiny Music is a free, ad-free Android player on YouTube Music's library, with synced lyrics,
offline downloads, device files, Spotify import and Listen Together. It exists to give Android
listeners a player that feels as considered as Apple Music, and to beat its upstream on
experience. Success: it feels premium and calm, starts fast, and never looks generated.

## Brand Personality
Premium, calm, confident. Apple Music in iOS 26/27 is the reference: neutral grounds, one tint
(Shiny Rose), large bold type, the Now Playing screen as the artwork's own world. The interface
steps back so the music and the cover lead.

## Anti-references
- Anything that reads as "AI-made": explanatory paragraphs on every screen, status badges,
  eyebrow labels everywhere, decorative glass, gradient text.
- Busy Material-era Android players with dense controls and chips.
- Settings pages that explain themselves instead of just offering the control.

## Design Principles
1. The music leads: artwork and lyrics first, chrome recedes until asked for.
2. Say it with the control, not with prose. No explanations where a switch or a clear label does.
3. Earned familiarity: behave like Apple Music where listeners already know the pattern.
4. Calm motion: slow, soft, exponential easing; nothing bounces; everything respects reduced motion.
5. Measured, not assumed: performance claims are phone-measured, and anything with no gain is removed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Text on artwork backdrops keeps at least 4.5:1 for body and 3:1 for large lyrics (scrims tuned per
artwork). Reduce Motion (the app's Instant transitions setting) disables drifting and fades.
Controls stay reachable by tap as well as gesture.
