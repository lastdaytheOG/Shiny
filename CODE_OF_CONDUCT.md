# Code of Conduct

Shiny is a small project, built in spare time by people who like music. These few rules keep it a place worth spending that time in. They apply to issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere else the project is talked about.

## How we work together

- **Talk about the work, not the person.** "This crashes on Android 11" is useful. "Whoever wrote this can't code" is not.
- **Assume the other person means well.** Many people here are writing in a second language, and tone gets lost in text.
- **Be patient with beginners.** Everyone's first bug report was missing something.
- **Take a no gracefully.** Not every idea fits, and a declined request isn't a judgement of you.
- **Give credit.** Say whose idea, code or report something came from.

## What isn't welcome

- Insults, mockery or harassment, in public or in private
- Remarks that demean people for who they are: their gender, sexuality, race, religion, nationality, age, disability or experience
- Sexual language or imagery
- Sharing someone's private details without their permission
- Spam, and pressure on maintainers to work faster

## When something goes wrong

Write to [hello@shinymusic.in](mailto:hello@shinymusic.in). Reports are read by the maintainer and kept private.

Depending on how serious it is, the response is a word in private, a warning, a temporary block, or a permanent one. Comments and contributions that break these rules may be edited or removed.

If you were on the receiving end of a decision and think it was wrong, reply to the same address and it will be looked at again.
