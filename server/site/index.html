VISilence · Privacy

Your music stays yours.

No ads, nothing sold, and no account to start. What you listen to is your business.

  • No ads, ever

    Shiny has no ads to show you, so there's nothing to target and no reason to profile you.

  • Never sold

    We don't sell your data to anyone. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers.

  • Kept on your phone

    Your history, playlists, likes and downloads live on your device, not on our servers.

  • No account needed

    Start listening without signing up. Sign in only if you want friends and a profile.

  • Anonymous diagnostics only

    Crash reports and basic app stats are anonymous, and never include what you play or search.
