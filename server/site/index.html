    Keep songs, albums and playlists for no signal.

  • Listen Together

  • Spotify import

    Bring your playlists over in a few taps.

    Bass, reverb, Slowed and 8D, all built in.

Version 1.2.8 · October 2026

Downloads that keep their covers offline, Autoplay without a connection, and your full Top Songs list.

New

  • Top Songs in Listening Stats opens the full list, with play counts.
  • Autoplay keeps going offline with your downloaded and on-device songs.

Improved

  • Downloaded songs keep their covers, even offline.
  • Less battery use with a Shiny widget on the home screen.
  • Shorter, simpler Settings, and Advanced is hidden.

Fixed

  • Song counts on Home and in Library now match their pages.
  • Signing in to YouTube no longer starts over on some phones.
  • Lists say “1 song” instead of “1 songs”.

Version 1.2.7 · October 2026

A calmer queue, and playlists that play all the way through.

New

  • Up Next is one list: what you played, what's playing and what's next.
  • Queue mode gives the list the whole screen on a dark background.
  • Every song in the queue has Play Next, Remove and Clear, with undo.

Improved

  • Long playlists load in full, even with Autoplay off.
  • Shuffle keeps its order as songs are added or load.
  • Shuffle, Repeat and Autoplay sit together at the top of the queue.
  • Long-press and drag to reorder songs.

Fixed

  • Songs were sometimes skipped or played twice when a playlist started while still loading.
  • The queue could show the same songs twice for a moment.

Version 1.2.6 · 22 October 2026

A cleaner Home and more dependable playback controls.

New

  • Hold and drag along the tab bar to move between tabs.

Improved

  • Home is cleaner: Your Week, Surprise Me and the mood picker are gone.
  • Shuffle, Repeat and Autoplay behave the same every time and stay in sync with the queue.
  • What's New no longer pops up after an update. It's in Settings → About.

Fixed

  • A crash in the lyrics view.
  • A crash when adding a song whose artist name has unusual characters to a playlist.

Version 1.2.5 · 10 October 2026

A small fix so Shiny can update itself again.

Fixed

  • The in-app update checker finds new versions again.

Improved

  • Faster APK downloads from this site.

Version 1.2.4 · 10 October 2026

A rebuilt Shiny: a new design on every screen, friends and Discord, and an appearance you can make your own.

New

  • Home, Library, Search, Now Playing and Settings rebuilt with one design.
  • Add friends, see what they're playing and let people listen along from Discord.
  • Appearance settings with a live preview: accent colour, glow, material and size.
  • A new panel to choose where music plays, including Bluetooth devices.
  • Charts follow your country, and Home learns from your very first play.
  • Smart Shuffle, a download folder you choose, and export to MP3.
  • Tap the time in the player to switch between time left and song length.

Improved

  • Shiny opens faster, and songs start playing sooner.
  • Crossfade waits until the next song is ready.
  • Update notifications open straight into Shiny.

Fixed

  • Lyrics stay in time after you seek.
  • Songs on your phone no longer skip when Shiny can't read them. It asks for access instead.
  • Sharing some playlists and albums no longer crashes the app.
  • Songs opened from another app no longer get replaced by your old queue.
